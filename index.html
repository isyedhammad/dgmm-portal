Official Data Reconciliation Findings (For Senior Leadership)

3 critical audit variances identified during database compilation across provincial spreadsheets
1. Chakwal Salt Lease Calculation Discrepancy (ML.CKL.I.R. SALT (11)): The spreadsheet summary row records PKR 13,053,450, but the mathematical sum of yearly columns (FY22–FY26) equals PKR 14,103,450 (understated by PKR 1,050,000).
2. Appellate Authority Docket Variance: The Appellate Authority summary sheet lists 294 pending appeals, but individual enumerated entries in the docket total 313 listed cases (19 appeals omitted from summary count).
3. EPA DAEC Scheduling Bottlenecks: 41 of 151 EPA cases (27.2%) have been stalled for >90 days awaiting District Administration Environmental Committee (DAEC) meetings convened by Deputy Commissioners in Rawalpindi, Chakwal, and Jhelum.
