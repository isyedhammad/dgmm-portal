Revenue Concentration Policy Insight

Geographic distribution of provincial reserve value

• Sargodha & Attock Drive 51.6% of Provincial Revenue: Together, Sargodha (Rs 9.42B) and Attock (Rs 7.97B) account for over half of Punjab's entire mining reserve price.

• Exceptional Revenue Density in Attock: While D.G. Khan and Rajanpur have larger geographic areas, Attock generates an unprecedented Rs 176,991 per acre, making it the most financially efficient mining district in Punjab.

• Sandstone Clusters: Sandstone accounts for 88 blocks commanding Rs 8.91 Billion in reserve value, with 52 of those blocks clustered in Sargodha.

Leadership Audit & Integrity Notes

Flagged discrepancies across provincial registers

• Chakwal Salt Lease Mismatch (ML.CKL.I.R. SALT (11)): Annual figures total Rs 14,103,450, but summary records Rs 13,053,450 (understated by Rs 1.05M).

• Appeals Docket Variance: Summary sheet lists 294 pending appeals, but docket enumerates 313 listed cases (19 uncounted in summary).

• DAEC Meeting Bottleneck: 41 of 151 EPA cases have been stalled for >90 days awaiting District Administration Environmental Committee (DAEC) sessions.
